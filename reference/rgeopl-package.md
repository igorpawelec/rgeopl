# rgeopl: Unified Access to Polish National Geodata (GUGiK and BDL)

Search and download spatial data from the Polish national geodetic
resource (GUGiK / PZGiK) and the Forest Data Bank (BDL, Lasy Panstwowe),
selected by an area of interest. Handles server-side record limits by
transparent pagination, keeps archival vintages as a queryable
attribute, and caches both index responses and downloaded files on disk.
